# Account & sign-in

**What it's for.** Nobody can submit, vote, or claim a page without an
account. This is the front door — Google or email, no password to manage.

**Where it stands.** Fully working. Both sign-in paths are live, and every
new sign-in gets a public profile automatically, with no separate signup
step.
